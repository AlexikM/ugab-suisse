On October 4, AGBU Switzerland had the pleasure of organizing a unique concert at Geneva's Saint Peter's Cathedral, in partnership with conductor **Luc Baghdassarian**.

The **5 recitatives by composer Shoujounian** and **Mozart's Requiem** echoed through the centuries-old stone walls, a subtle reminder of the resilience of the Armenian people throughout the ages.

The evening continued with an informal dinner bringing together the AGBU Switzerland committee, as well as **Sam Simonian**, President of AGBU Global, and Board Members **Vartkess Knadjian** and **Nazareth Festekjian**.

A heartfelt thank-you to everyone who took part in this celebration of music and Armenian resilience.
